# ROAS & Ad ROI Calculator — Break-Even & Net Profit Estimator

A lightweight, real-time web calculator for digital marketers, media buyers, and e-commerce store owners to calculate **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)**, **Net Campaign Profit**, **True Marketing ROI**, **CPA**, and **Break-Even ROAS** based on product profit margins.

👉 **Live Interactive Tool:** https://valeryivch-maker.github.io/roas-roi-calculator/

## Key Features
- **True Profitability & Break-Even Math:** Instantly determines the exact minimum ROAS (`1 ÷ Profit Margin`) required before scaling paid traffic on Meta, Google, or TikTok Ads.
- **Industry Presets:** One-click templates for E-Commerce Stores, SaaS / Digital Products, Affiliate / Lead Gen, and High-Ticket offers.
- **Shareable Campaign Links:** Use **Copy Estimate Link** to generate pre-filled URLs (`?spend=...&rev=...&margin=...&conv=...`) for client reports and media buying audits.
- **Zero Tracking of Financial Inputs:** Pure client-side HTML/CSS/JS execution with instant recalculation.

## URL Query Parameters (Pre-filled Scenarios)
- `spend` — Total Ad Spend in USD (e.g., `1000`)
- `rev` — Total Revenue Generated in USD (e.g., `4200`)
- `margin` — Product Profit Margin percentage (e.g., `60`)
- `conv` — Total Conversions / Sales count (e.g., `85`)
