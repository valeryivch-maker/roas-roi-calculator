ROAS vs. ROI: What Is the Difference?

ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) measures gross revenue generated for every dollar spent on advertising (Revenue ÷ Ad Spend). For example, spending $1,000 to generate $4,200 in sales equals a 4.20x ROAS.

True Marketing ROI accounts for your product profit margin and cost of goods sold (COGS). Knowing your Break-Even ROAS (1 ÷ Profit Margin) tells you the exact minimum return required before an ad campaign starts losing money.
